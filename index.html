A little more about me.

Background

I’m a Mathematics major at IISER Bhopal with a deep-rooted passion for aviation. My interest started with aerodynamics—realizing that the complex math I study is the exact same math that keeps aircraft in the sky. That curiosity drove me to design and build my own RC plane from scratch.

What I do now

Right now, I’m focused on building a strong foundation in applied mathematics and physics while pursuing independent aerospace projects. I spend my free time researching flight mechanics, working on aerodynamic designs, and connecting theoretical math to real-world engineering.

What I’m into

I am fascinated by aircraft design and the computational side of aviation. I’m a huge fan of the engineering behind the B-787 and love tracking flights on Flightradar24. Ultimately, I’m working toward a long-term career in aerospace engineering and space tech.
